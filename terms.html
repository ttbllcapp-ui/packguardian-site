The service

Pack Guardian lets you track packages from multiple carriers in one app, receive status updates, and prepare claim letters for lost or damaged shipments. Full use of the app requires a Pack Guardian Pro subscription, described below.

Subscription

Pack Guardian Pro is an auto-renewing weekly subscription purchased through your Apple ID. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial; the price shown in the App Store at checkout applies after the trial ends. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase, and the subscription renews automatically each week unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your device's Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions. We use RevenueCat to process subscription status; see our Privacy Policy for details.

Accuracy

Tracking information comes from carriers and third-party data providers. We work to keep it accurate and timely, but we cannot guarantee it. Always confirm critical delivery details with your carrier.

Claim letters

Claim letters and complaint texts are drafts prepared for your convenience. You are responsible for reviewing them before sending. Pack Guardian does not guarantee any claim outcome and is not a party to disputes between you and a carrier.
