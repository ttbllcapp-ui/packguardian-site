The service

Pack Guardian lets you track packages from multiple carriers in one app, receive status updates, and prepare claim letters for lost or damaged shipments. On a new install, using the app requires a Guardian Premium subscription or lifetime purchase, described below.

Guardian Premium

Guardian Premium is offered as:

  • Weekly: an auto-renewing subscription. Eligible new subscribers get a free trial, currently 3 days. The trial length is set in the App Store and shown in the app before you confirm.
  • Monthly: an auto-renewing subscription.
  • Yearly: an auto-renewing subscription. When you close the plan screen, the app may offer a yearly plan at a discounted price. This offer is a separate yearly subscription in the same subscription group; its price and saving are shown before you confirm.
  • Lifetime: a one-time purchase that never renews.

Prices for your country are shown in the app and in the App Store before you buy. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account when you confirm the purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically at the same price and for the same period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. If you start a free trial, the subscription price is charged when the trial ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription.

You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID account settings (on iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions). Deleting the app or your Pack Guardian account does not cancel a subscription. We use RevenueCat to check subscription status; see our Privacy Policy for details.

If you used Pack Guardian 1.0 or 1.0.1 before Guardian Premium was introduced, the features you use today stay free for you; Premium is optional.

Purchases are also subject to Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

Accuracy

Tracking information comes from carriers through our tracking data provider, 17TRACK. Availability depends on the carrier. When live tracking data is not available for a package, the app says so; it does not show estimated or sample events in its place. We work to keep tracking information accurate and timely, but we cannot guarantee it. Guardian AI answers are written by AI and can be wrong. Always confirm critical delivery details with your carrier.

Claim letters

Claim letters and complaint texts are drafts prepared for your convenience. You are responsible for reviewing them before sending. Pack Guardian does not guarantee any claim outcome and is not a party to disputes between you and a carrier.
