Privacy Policy

Pack Guardian. Last updated: October 6, 2026

Summary

Pack Guardian is a package tracking app with an optional Pro subscription. We collect the minimum data needed to run the service. We do not sell your data. We do not show ads.

What we collect

  • Account: by default we create an anonymous account with a random identifier when you first open the app. You can instead create an account with an email and password, or continue with Apple; in that case we store the email and name you provide.
  • Tracking data: the tracking numbers you add, package nicknames, categories, and declared values you enter.
  • Optional photos: if you use the unboxing photo feature, the photo is stored with your package record.
  • Preferences: app language and notification settings.
  • Purchases: if you subscribe to Pack Guardian Pro, Apple and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, process the purchase and keep your subscription active or expired status. RevenueCat identifies you by a randomly generated identifier, not your name or email.
  • AI assistant questions: if you use the AI assistant or Claim Shield features, the question you ask and the relevant package details are sent to our AI provider solely to generate that response.

How we use it

  • To show tracking status for the packages you add.
  • To generate claim letters and delivery answers you request.
  • To remember your settings between sessions.
  • To activate and maintain your Pro subscription.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not show third-party ads.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Third parties

To provide live tracking, tracking numbers you add may be sent to carrier data providers solely to retrieve shipment status. Tracking numbers are not linked to your identity. Subscription purchases are processed by Apple and RevenueCat. AI assistant and Claim Shield requests are processed through Emergent's AI gateway, which forwards them to Anthropic's Claude models to generate a response; this data is used only to generate that response.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings, Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
